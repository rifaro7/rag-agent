Summer Intern Onboarding Checklist

Day 1: Interns receive a temporary laptop and are added to the intern Slack
channel only, not the full company workspace. Security training is
completed within the first week.

Week 1: Each intern is paired with a buddy (not a formal mentor) who checks
in twice a week for the first month.

Final review: Interns receive a single end-of-internship review at the
10-week mark, which informs return-offer decisions.

Interns are expected to complete one small project by the end of the
internship, not a solo production feature.
