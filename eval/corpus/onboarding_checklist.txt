New Engineer Onboarding Checklist

Day 1: IT provisions a laptop and creates accounts for email, Slack, and the
internal wiki. New hires complete security training within the first 3 days.

Week 1: A mentor is assigned from within the new hire's team. The mentor and
new hire meet daily for the first two weeks, then move to a weekly check-in.

30-60-90 review: Managers hold a formal check-in at the 30-day, 60-day, and
90-day marks. The 90-day review determines whether the initial probationary
period is completed successfully.

Engineers are expected to ship their first small pull request within the
first two weeks and their first solo feature within the first quarter.
